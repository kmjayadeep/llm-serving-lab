What stands out

The 0.5B model sustains roughly four times the per-request decode rate of the 3B model. Aggregate throughput continues to rise through concurrency 16, but both models show a clear TTFT and latency penalty at that level.

Method

Closed-loop streaming workload with 75 prompt tokens, 128 output tokens, three warm-ups, and 50 measured requests per concurrency. Model startup is excluded. See phase1-baseline.md and the raw JSONL files for details.
